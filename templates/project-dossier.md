## Calculation and margins

Attach flow schematic with boundaries and numbered segments. Record each segment's Q/A/v/Re/f/L/D/roughness/K/source and losses. Equipment losses identify reference flow/density and clean/dirty state. Show system curves, vendor curves, intersections and power envelope. Separate leakage, future duty, fouling, installation effects and uncertainty. Document each allowance once and its owner. Attach sensitivity cases and limitations.

## Vendor technical bid comparison

Compare candidates on identical actual gas/duty bases: guaranteed performance, allowed range, material/temperature capability, certification scope, full power curve, motor/drive, sound spectrum, vibration/balance method, test plan, maintainability, spares and life-cycle energy assumptions. Mark deviations explicitly. Reject unsupported guarantees or unresolved mandatory requirements before purchase, not through an invented weighted score.

## Commissioning and operation

Record rotation, speed, density, airflow by branch, pressure taps, filter state, electrical measurements, vibration locations/bands, sound receiver and control setpoints. Verify low-flow alarm, dirty-filter alarm, safe shutdown and process interlocks where specified. Capture testing must reflect actual contaminant source and make-up air. Issue baseline readings, inspection intervals, cleaning/maintenance criteria, spare-parts list and retraining needs. Assign the owner of recurring examination per jurisdiction.
