# International basis, European Union and Brazil annexes

Review date 2026-10-13. This annex defines screening tasks and evidence; it is not a legal opinion or declaration of conformity. Determine site, product scope, responsible economic operator, supply date and integration status before selecting obligations.

## International projects

ISO, AMCA/ASHRAE and IEC provide technical frameworks; they are not all mandatory everywhere. Use the host country's law, contractual specification and applicable national adoptions. Define an engineering authority to resolve conflicts and keep a deviation register. A tested fan does not establish the effectiveness of an entire LEV installation. Identify occupational exposure, fire, environmental emissions, structural and electrical requirements independently.

## European Union / Spain

**Fan ecodesign.** Screen [Regulation (EU) 2024/1834](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1834), its [2025/2481 amendment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32025R2481), and the [consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1834-20251228). The framework concerns fans driven by motors with electric input power 125 W to 500 kW, subject to definitions/exclusions. General application is from 24 July 2026; the amendment includes transitional provisions for integrated and replacement fans, so do not use one blanket date for all products. Verify the precise category, transition and current corrigenda in the official text. The sample shaft power alone does not establish scope, efficiency compliance or an exemption. Request supplier calculations, product information and the appropriate conformity documentation. Verification tolerances in market surveillance are not extra design allowances.

**Machinery.** Determine whether the delivery is complete machinery, partly completed machinery or an integrated assembly. Assess Directive 2006/42/EC and the transition to [Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj), whose general application date is 20 January 2027 as corrected by the [official corrigendum](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32023R1230R(01)). Verify exceptions and dates for the actual product. Record risk assessment, instructions, technical documentation and the declaration appropriate to the supply boundary. A CE-marked motor does not establish assembly conformity.

**Explosive atmospheres.** Screen [Directive 2014/34/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/34/oj) for equipment and protective systems and workplace requirements under [Directive 1999/92/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1999/92/oj), with national transposition. Obtain zone classification, gas/dust group, temperature limits, ignition-risk assessment and an appropriate whole-assembly protection strategy. Verify applicable EN fan/ignition standards through official national catalogs and harmonized-standard listings; no unverified EN edition or certification is asserted here.

**Other obligations.** Review electrical safety, EMC, motor/drive ecodesign, occupational exposure/noise, fire/smoke extraction and environmental permitting as triggered. In Spain, confirm national transposition, competent-authority and installation requirements; do not assume the EU product framework replaces site obligations. Assign legal/product-compliance review before purchase release.

## Brazil

**Machine safety.** Use the current official [NR-12 page and controlled text](https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/participacao-social/conselhos-e-orgaos-colegiados/comissao-tripartite-partitaria-permanente/normas-regulamentadora/normas-regulamentadoras-vigentes/norma-regulamentadora-no-12-nr-12). Assess rotating parts, guarding, access, safe maintenance, controls and instructions. A guard in an illustration is not a documented NR-12 assessment.

**Electrical safety.** Use the current official [NR-10 page](https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/participacao-social/conselhos-e-orgaos-colegiados/comissao-tripartite-partitaria-permanente/normas-regulamentadora/normas-regulamentadoras-vigentes/norma-regulamentadora-no-10-nr-10). Verify the operative text and transition provisions at release; do not freeze an older edition during a regulatory revision. Obtain electrical design/protection, earthing, isolation and authorized maintenance requirements applicable to the installation.

**Exposure control.** The official [NR-9 page](https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/participacao-social/conselhos-e-orgaos-colegiados/comissao-tripartite-partitaria-permanente/normas-regulamentadora/normas-regulamentadoras-vigentes/norma-regulamentadora-no-9-nr-9) identifies evaluation/control of physical, chemical and biological occupational exposures and lists a January 2026 amendment. Integrate LEV performance with the site's risk-management and occupational-hygiene process. Airflow alone does not prove exposure control. Evaluate additional applicable NRs for the actual activity; do not add NR-13 merely because a fan has a pressure rise.

**National standards and responsibility.** Verify applicable ABNT NBR/IEC/ISO adoptions and current editions in the official ABNT catalog; do not invent national equivalents or year suffixes. Evaluate local fire-department requirements, emissions licensing, hazardous-area equipment certification and professional-responsibility/ART requirements with the responsible organization and qualified professional. The software does not issue ART or validate professional authority.

## Required applicability record

For every requirement record: jurisdiction; product/installation boundary; identifier; edition/amendments; legal or contractual trigger; applicable/not applicable/pending; justification; exact clause if checked; evidence ID; responsible reviewer; date; and open action. Unresolved mandatory applicability remains a purchase-release hold point, while preliminary calculations may continue.
