# Standards register and application guide

Checked 2026-10-13. This is an applicability guide based on official catalogs/public scopes, with engineering recommendations for evidence. It is not a clause-by-clause reproduction or audit of every licensed standard. Do not invent clause numbers, tolerances, vibration grades or certification. Recheck editions, amendments, errata, adoption and contract requirements at project kickoff and before release.

## Evidence classifications

**Catalog verified:** identifier/scope checked, full clauses not assessed. **Public method reviewed:** relevant public technical guidance consulted, not a complete conformity assessment. **Project adopted:** client has selected edition and applicability. **Clause verified:** named competent reviewer has checked the licensed text and recorded clause/evidence. A catalog-verified item cannot be promoted automatically to clause verified.

| ID / edition observed | Purpose and application | Evidence to request / boundary |

| ISO 13349-1:2022 | Fan terminology; avoid inconsistent definitions in specification | Agreed terminology and pressure/power boundaries; not electrical safety |
| ISO 5801:2017 + Amd 1:2025 | Aerodynamic performance testing in standardized airways; conversions and uncertainty within its limits | Test report, arrangement, density, speed, measured quantities and uncertainty; does not prove installed capture |

| AMCA Publication 201-23 | Application guide for fan/system interaction and installation effects | Layout review and justified system-effect correction; guidance is not universal statutory law |
| AMCA Publication 203-90 (R2011) | Field fan-system measurement guidance | Field plan, measurement sections and correction basis; catalog marks it under review |
| ISO 5802:2001, including applicable amendment | In-situ fan performance testing | Adopted edition/amendments, site method and uncertainty; lab ratings and site tests have different conditions |
| AMCA 803-26 | Site performance testing for industrial process/power-generation fans | Decide whether service fits scope; agreed test plan and guarantees |

| ISO 12499:1999 / AMCA Publication 410-25 | Fan mechanical guarding and safety practices | Risk assessment, guards, safe access and maintenance instructions; not a complete electrical or explosion-protection assessment |

AMCA entries are catalog-verified through the [official publication register](https://www.amca.org/publish/). AMCA 201/203/207/208 and 311 were marked under review; this does not by itself withdraw the listed edition. Do not infer edition from a legacy URL slug.

## How to use the standards in a project

1. Establish country, equipment function, new/existing status and date of placing on market or commissioning. Separate legislation, contract, product standard, test method and design guide.
2. Select one coherent aerodynamic guarantee method and pressure/power definition. State the test arrangement and correction rules. Record why alternatives are or are not accepted.
3. Obtain controlled copies of adopted standards, amendments and errata. Map applicable clauses to calculations, vendor documents or inspections. A source link is not evidence that a clause was met.
4. Agree numerical criteria, tolerances and uncertainty treatment with the client/vendor. Do not borrow statutory market-surveillance tolerances as allowable design error.
5. Resolve conflicts through the responsible project authority; mandatory law cannot be waived by a vendor deviation. A new edition does not silently override the contracted edition; assess legal and contractual implications.
